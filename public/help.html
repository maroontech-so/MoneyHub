Frequently Asked Questions

How do rewards work?

Complete available tasks, surveys, or AI training modules. Once your submission is reviewed and approved by the system, the potential reward is credited to your available balance. This is subject to quality checks.

How do withdrawals work?

You can withdraw your available balance directly to your M-Pesa number. Withdrawals are typically processed within a few minutes. Minimum withdrawal limits may apply based on your account level.

M-Pesa Troubleshooting

Ensure the phone number provided in your profile matches the M-Pesa registered number. If a withdrawal fails, the funds will be returned to your PesaWave wallet automatically.

Account Security

Never share your password or OTPs. PesaWave support will never ask for your password. Enable two-factor authentication if available in your region.
